## Writing

- **[ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com)** — General chat, drafting, brainstorming, and light research in one place. **Free:** text chat on the free model tier; caps on advanced features; no card required for basic use.
- **[Claude](https://claude.ai)** — Strong long-form drafting and careful reasoning with a clean chat UI. **Free:** daily message limits on the free model; projects/artifacts may be capped.
- **[Gemini](https://gemini.google.com)** — Writing plus research, images, and Google Workspace tie-ins in one free stack. **Free:** generous access to Flash/Pro-class models, Live voice, and image gen with daily caps.
- **[Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com)** — Grammar, clarity, and tone suggestions while you write. **Free:** core writing suggestions; AI prompt quota limited on free.
- **<a href="https://rytr.me?via=chad-straight" rel="sponsored nofollow">Rytr</a>** — Short-form copy templates (ads, bios, emails) with a character budget. **Free:** monthly free character allotment; enough for light personal use.
- **[Writesonic](https://writesonic.com)** — Marketing copy and SEO-oriented drafts with a free starter allowance. **Free:** limited words/credits on the free plan.

## Image

- **[Adobe Firefly](https://firefly.adobe.com)** — Text-to-image and generative fill with commercially oriented training claims. **Free:** monthly generative credits; check commercial terms per model before client work.
- **[Microsoft Designer](https://designer.microsoft.com)** / **[Bing Image Creator](https://www.bing.com/images/create)** — Quick social graphics and image gen via Microsoft account. **Free:** boosts / daily generation limits.
- **[Recraft](https://www.recraft.ai)** — Images plus vector/SVG-friendly outputs for design work. **Free:** daily free generations; higher fidelity on paid.
- **[Canva](https://www.canva.com)** — Design suite with Magic Studio AI for non-designers. **Free:** always-free editor; AI uses and premium assets capped / locked.
- **[remove.bg](https://www.remove.bg)** — One-click background removal for product and portrait shots. **Free:** limited free previews/downloads at lower resolution. *(Affiliate program status may change during Canva migration — verify before promoting.)*

## Video

- **[Google Flow](https://flow.google.com)** — Easiest free route to Google Veo-class clips with synced audio. **Free:** daily Flow credits (a few short clips); quality depends on credit spend.
- **[Kling AI](https://kling.ai)** — Competitive text/image-to-video with a free credit pool. **Free:** starter credits that refill slowly; commercial rights may need paid.
- **[CapCut](https://www.capcut.com)** — Full free video editor (cuts, captions, effects) for Shorts/Reels. **Free:** editor always free; some templates/effects watermark or need Pro.
- **[VEED](https://www.veed.io)** — Browser editor with AI subtitles, cleanup, and avatars. **Free:** watermarked / limited minutes.
- **[Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com)** — Collaborative online editor (resize, captions, meme tools). **Free:** exports with watermark / resolution limits. *(Kapwing affiliate pending — plain link for now.)*
- **[InVideo](https://invideo.io)** — Template-first AI video maker from scripts or prompts. **Free:** watermarked videos and limited AI generations.
- **[OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro)** — Auto-clips long videos into Shorts/Reels with titles. **Free:** monthly clip credits; free clips often watermarked and expire.

## Audio

- **[ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io)** — Best-in-class TTS and voice cloning for demos and accessibility. **Free:** ~10k credits/month; **no commercial license on Free**. *(ElevenLabs affiliate pending — plain link for now.)*
- **[Murf](https://www.murf.ai)** — Studio-style AI voiceovers for videos and courses. **Free:** limited free minutes/voices; export limits on free. *(Murf affiliate pending — plain link for now.)*
- **[Speechify](https://speechify.com)** — Listen to articles, PDFs, and docs with natural voices. **Free:** basic listening with caps; Premium unlocks quality/speed.
- **[Suno](https://suno.com)** — Text-to-song / music generation that’s fun and fast. **Free:** daily credits; free-tier songs typically **non-commercial / limited downloads**.
- **[Whisper](https://github.com/openai/whisper)** — Open-source speech-to-text you can run locally. **Free:** fully free/local (your hardware).

## Coding

- **[GitHub Copilot Free](https://github.com/features/copilot)** — Completions + chat/agent in major IDEs. **Free:** ~2,000 completions and ~50 chat/agent requests per month.
- **[Cursor](https://cursor.com)** — AI-first code editor with strong agent workflows. **Free:** Hobby plan with limited slow premium requests.
- **[Windsurf](https://windsurf.com)** — Agent-style IDE assistant with a free tier. **Free:** limited prompt credits.
- **[Continue](https://continue.dev)** — Open-source IDE extension; bring your own free API keys or local models. **Free:** extension free; model cost depends on what you connect.
- **[Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com)** — Free hosted notebooks with GPU slices for ML experiments. **Free:** limited GPU sessions; disconnects under load.
- **[Groq](https://groq.com)** — Extremely fast inference playground/API for open models. **Free:** rate-limited free API/console usage.
- **[Ollama](https://ollama.com)** — Run Llama, Mistral, etc. locally with a simple CLI/API. **Free:** fully free software; you supply the machine.

## Productivity

- **[NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com)** — Ground answers and audio overviews on *your* uploaded sources. **Free:** Google account; notebook/source caps on free.
- **[Gamma](https://gamma.app)** — AI decks and docs from a prompt — fast first drafts. **Free:** limited AI credits; exports may be watermarked or capped.
- **[Descript](https://www.descript.com)** — Edit podcasts/videos like a doc (transcript-based). **Free:** limited transcription minutes; free exports often watermarked/720p.
- **[Otter.ai](https://otter.ai)** — Meeting transcription and summaries. **Free:** monthly transcription minutes; import/export limits.
- **[Fireflies.ai](https://fireflies.ai)** — Meeting notetaker that joins calls and summarizes. **Free:** limited storage/transcription; team features locked.

## Research

- **[Consensus](https://consensus.app)** — Answers backed by peer-reviewed papers with citations. **Free:** limited deep searches / AI features on free.
- **[Elicit](https://elicit.com)** — Literature review workflows (find papers, extract data). **Free:** basic searches and limited extractions per month.
- **[Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org)** — AI-augmented academic search with TL;DRs. **Free:** fully free research search.
- **[Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com)** — Free developer playground for Gemini models/APIs. **Free:** generous free quota for experimentation (not unlimited prod traffic).
- **[LM Arena](https://lmarena.ai)** — Blind-compare chat models side-by-side. **Free:** fully free comparison arena.
